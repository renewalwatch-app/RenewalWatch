Privacy policy

Last updated November 2026

RenewalWatch helps businesses track IT assets and renewal dates. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

Your email address and a password (stored hashed by our authentication provider). The inventory data you enter, such as item names, serial numbers, dates, costs and who items are assigned to. Billing details are collected and stored by Stripe; we never see your full card number.

How we use it

To run the service, send the reminder emails you set up, and handle billing. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with our service providers: Supabase (database and sign-in), Vercel (hosting), Resend (email delivery) and Stripe (payments). Some of these providers may store data outside Canada, including in the United States.

Your choices

You can export your data at any time from Settings. To delete your account and data, email us and we will remove it within 30 days.

Please don't store secrets

Do not enter passwords, license keys or other credentials. Use a password manager for those.
