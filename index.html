Questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in your browser. There are no agents and no network scans. You add items by hand or import a spreadsheet.

Do you store passwords or license keys?

No, and we ask you not to. Keep secrets in a password manager. RenewalWatch tracks dates, owners, costs and details like hostnames and seat counts.

Can my team use it?

Yes, on Pro. Invite colleagues by email and everyone sees the same inventory. Reminders can go to a shared inbox like it@yourcompany.ca.

Can I bring my existing spreadsheet?

Yes, on Pro. Download the import template, paste in your rows and upload it.

What happens if I cancel Pro?

Nothing is deleted. You keep read and edit access to everything. Adding new items beyond the free limit, or adding Pro categories, needs Pro again.
