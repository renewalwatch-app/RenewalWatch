Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the operator of RenewalWatch ("RenewalWatch", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, discloses and safeguards personal information in connection with the website at renewalwatch.ca and the RenewalWatch web application (the "Service"). We handle personal information in accordance with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act ("PIPEDA") and other applicable privacy laws. By using the Service, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Use of Artificial Intelligence

The Service, including its software code and this Privacy Policy, was created in whole or in part with the assistance of artificial intelligence tools. We do not use artificial intelligence services to analyze, profile or make decisions about you, and we do not send Your Data to artificial intelligence providers as part of operating the Service. The security and privacy measures described below are provided on a reasonable-efforts basis, and no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure or error-free.

2. Information We Collect

Account information. Your email address, a password (stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider), your workspace or company name, and the email addresses of team members you invite.

Information you enter ("Your Data"). Details of IT assets, software, subscriptions, domains, certificates, contracts, dates, costs, locations, notes and uploaded files, which may include the names of employees or other individuals to whom equipment or licenses are assigned. You are responsible for ensuring you have the right to provide any personal information about other individuals.

Billing information. Payments are processed by Stripe. We receive limited information such as your customer identifier, subscription status and billing email. We do not receive or store your full payment card number.

Technical information. Our service providers automatically process technical data such as IP addresses, browser and device type, and request logs to operate, secure and troubleshoot the Service. The Service stores a sign-in token and basic preferences in your browser's local storage so that you remain signed in.

Analytics. We use Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google LLC, to understand how visitors use the website and the Service, such as pages viewed, buttons clicked, approximate location (country or city), device and browser type, and referring websites. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies, and the information it collects is processed by Google, including in the United States. We have disabled Google's advertising features and ad personalization for our analytics, and we do not send your name, email address, or the content of Your Data to Google Analytics. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, by blocking cookies in your browser, or by using a browser or extension that blocks analytics. We do not use advertising cookies.

3. How We Use Information

We use personal information only for the purposes of: (a) providing, operating and maintaining the Service; (b) authenticating users and securing accounts; (c) sending reminders, digests, account and service-related emails you have requested or that are necessary to provide the Service; (d) processing subscriptions and payments; (e) responding to inquiries and support requests; (f) detecting, preventing and addressing fraud, abuse, security incidents and technical issues; (g) understanding how the Service is used so that we can improve it; and (h) complying with legal obligations. We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Disclosure to Service Providers

We share personal information with trusted service providers only as necessary to operate the Service, including providers of website hosting, database and file storage, user authentication, email delivery, payment processing and website analytics. These providers are permitted to use personal information only to perform services on our behalf and are expected to protect it. We may also disclose information where required or permitted by law, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or in connection with a sale, merger or reorganization of the Service, in which case the recipient will be bound to handle the information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

5. International Storage and Transfer

Our service providers may store and process personal information in Canada, the United States and other countries. Information stored outside Canada is subject to the laws of those jurisdictions and may be accessible to their courts, law enforcement and national security authorities.

6. Retention

We retain personal information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service. If you request deletion of your account, we will delete or anonymize Your Data within a reasonable period, generally within thirty (30) days, except where retention is required by law or for legitimate purposes such as billing records, dispute resolution or enforcement of our agreements. Residual copies may remain in backups maintained by our service providers for a limited period.

7. Safeguards

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, access controls that restrict each workspace's data to its members, and private file storage. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your login credentials confidential and for not storing passwords, license keys, payment card numbers, government identification numbers or other sensitive information in the Service.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you, and you may withdraw your consent to our handling of it, subject to legal or contractual restrictions and reasonable notice. Withdrawing consent may mean we can no longer provide the Service to you. You can export Your Data at any time from the Settings page, and you can change reminder settings or unsubscribe from reminder emails by adjusting your settings or deleting your account. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

9. Breach Notification

If we become aware of a breach of security safeguards involving personal information under our control that creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify affected individuals and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as required by law.

10. Children

The Service is intended for business use and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of majority.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy on this page and update the effective date above, and where changes are material we will make reasonable efforts to notify you. Your continued use of the Service after the update constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

12. Contact and Complaints

Questions, requests or complaints about this Privacy Policy or our handling of personal information may be sent to our privacy contact at support@renewalwatch.ca. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at www.priv.gc.ca.
